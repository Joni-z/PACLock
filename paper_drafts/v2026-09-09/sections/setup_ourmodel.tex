\paragraph{Our model.}
Both CroFreMo encoders are trained with AdamW (learning rate $10^{-4}$,
weight decay $10^{-5}$), cosine decay, gradient clipping at 1.0, batch
size 32, and the loss of the corpus (cross-entropy with label smoothing
0.1 for multi-class tasks, binary cross-entropy or focal loss for the two
seizure corpora that use them), with the number of epochs and evaluation
schedule matched to the from-scratch baselines of the same corpus. Model
selection uses the validation split only; the test split is scored once,
at the selected checkpoint. The recipe was fixed for the tri-axial encoder
and inherited unchanged by the axis-free one. The pretrained row of the
tri-axial model uses the checkpoint of Section~\ref{sec:objective} at 60k
steps and the standard finetuning recipe for pretrained EEG models: base
learning rate $5\times10^{-4}$ with layer-wise decay 0.65 by depth, two
warmup epochs, and two initial epochs in which only the tensors not
carried by the checkpoint are trained before the whole model is unfrozen;
this recipe is fixed once for all corpora. The axis-free encoder has no
pretrained row: its block structure does not match the checkpoint, and we
did not pretrain it separately.

\paragraph{Seeds and reporting.}
Every baseline cell and every tri-axial CroFreMo cell is the mean and
standard deviation over three seeds. The axis-free CroFreMo cells and the
CBraMod transplant cells are single seeds, marked $(1)$ throughout, and
will be replaced by three-seed values; the seed standard deviations we
measure on the tri-axial model with the same recipe (0.01--0.05 depending
on the corpus, Table~\ref{tab:main}) are the reference against which the
single-seed differences should be read. The primary metric per corpus
follows the established convention for that task: AUC-PR for the three
seizure corpora, Cohen's $\kappa$ for TUEV, IIIC, TUAR and the two sleep
corpora, balanced accuracy for TUAB, ADFD and CAUEEG, and AUROC for TUEP;
balanced accuracy, weighted F1 and AUROC are reported alongside in the
appendix. Learned gate values ($\boldsymbol{\beta}$, $\gamma$ of
Eq.~(\ref{eq:duplex})) are reported per corpus as measurements of how much
coupling each task retained.
