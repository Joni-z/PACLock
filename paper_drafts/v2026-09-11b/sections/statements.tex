\subsection*{AI use statement}
Large language models were used as a coding and writing assistant: to
implement experiment configurations and cluster job scripts from the
authors' specifications, to maintain the experiment log, and to draft and
edit prose under the authors' direction. Every experimental design
decision, every number reported, and every claim made was specified,
checked, and approved by the authors, who take full responsibility for
the content.

\subsection*{Ethics statement}
All corpora are publicly available, de-identified clinical EEG datasets
used under their respective data-use agreements; no new data were
collected. The models are research artifacts and are not intended for
clinical decision-making.

\subsection*{Reproducibility statement}
Preprocessing scripts regenerate every processed corpus from the raw
download with a manifest recording versions, split membership and raw-file
checksums (Section~\ref{sec:setup}). Model code, all configuration files
(including every variant of Table~\ref{tab:variants-all} and every
baseline recipe), and per-run result files will be released. Single-seed
cells are marked as such throughout and will be replaced by three-seed
values.
