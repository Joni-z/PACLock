\section{Pretraining the tri-axial model: an exploratory result}
\label{app:pretrain}

The tri-axial model was pretrained with the objective of
Section~\ref{sec:objective} on a pool of the twelve corpora's training
splits plus a 2,000-hour, subject-diverse slice of TUEG with every session
shared with TUSZ removed, for 60k steps, and finetuned under the fixed
recipe of Section~\ref{sec:setup}. Table~\ref{tab:pretrain-triaxial}
gives the outcome next to the from-scratch model. The pretrained model
improves where labels are scarce or positives rare (Siena, TUSZ,
Sleep-EDF) and loses where labels are plentiful (TUEV, IIIC, TUEP, TUAR,
ADFD, CAUEEG, ISRUC). Frozen-probe and label-fraction analyses that would
support a mechanistic reading were run but are not reported here, because
their settings differ from the finetuning protocol of this table and we
have not yet reconciled them; the pattern is offered as an observation
about a small, strongly biased model under a reconstruction objective,
not as a property of the objective. The axis-free model has no
pretrained row: its block structure does not match the checkpoint and we
did not pretrain it separately.
